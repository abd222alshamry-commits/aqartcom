# V32 — Electronic Invoicing
- Central electronic invoices for hotels, real-estate offices and customers.
- Invoice / credit note / debit note document types.
- Sequential unique invoice identifiers, issue/due dates, tax fields and payment status.
- Line items with quantity, unit price, tax rate, subtotal/tax/total calculations.
- QR payload and PNG endpoint.
- PDF invoice endpoint using PDFKit.
- Partial/full payment registration and references.
- Admin tab: الفوترة الإلكترونية.

Note: this is an application invoicing engine. Country-specific tax authority clearance/signing (e.g. ZATCA Phase 2) requires onboarding credentials, cryptographic signing and authority-specific XML/UBL integration before claiming regulatory compliance.
