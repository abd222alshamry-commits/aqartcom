# V34 — Executive Business Intelligence

- Executive dashboard for 7/30/90/365 day windows.
- Revenue, expenses, profit, revenue growth and a transparent run-rate 30-day forecast.
- Hotel bookings/value, completed real-estate deals/value, active properties/hotels/offices/users.
- 12-month revenue/expense trend.
- Top cities, hotels and real-estate offices.
- Management alerts for pending properties, unpaid invoices, open hotel payouts and cancelled bookings.
- Endpoint: GET /api/admin/executive-bi?days=30

Forecast is intentionally labeled as a simple run-rate estimate, not guaranteed AI prediction.
