# عقارتكم V27 — Hotel Payments & Collection

- Links OTA booking → financial record → invoice → payout/guest collection ledger.
- Records actual OTA payouts and guest payments.
- Automatically calculates payout and collection variances.
- Creates discrepancy alerts and supports resolving reviewed alerts.
- Adds a hotel partner Payments & Collection dashboard.
- Booking.com payout expectations come from the V26 Payments API integration.
- Agoda/Expedia actual financial endpoints remain contract-specific.
